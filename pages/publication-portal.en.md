# Publication Portal

The contributions in the “Publications Portal: European Peace Treaties” were published between 2008 and 2010 as part of the DFG project “European Peace Treaties of the Pre-Modern Era Online.” They present findings from historical peace research on the pre-modern period, viewed from diverse disciplinary perspectives (including history, international law, political science, linguistics, and art history).

Publications Portal: European Peace Treaties, edited by the Leibniz Institute for European History (IEG), Mainz.
ISSN: 1867-9714
URL: https://www.ieg-friedensvertraege.de/publikationsportal/
URN: urn:nbn:de:0159-2009011407
