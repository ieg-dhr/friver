### Cookies

When visiting individual pages, so-called temporary cookies are used. These session cookies do not contain any personal data and are automatically deleted when you close your web browser at the end of your session.

### Statistical Analysis with Matomo

Some websites of the IEG Mainz, including this one, use Matomo — an open-source software for statistical analysis of visitor traffic. The IP address is anonymized immediately after processing and before storage. All collected data is stored on a server of Johannes Gutenberg University Mainz in Germany.

Anonymized data is used exclusively for statistical purposes and to ensure proper operation of the website. No personal user profiles are created. Data is not shared with third parties. This website does not employ any technologies designed to analyze the browsing behavior of individual users.
